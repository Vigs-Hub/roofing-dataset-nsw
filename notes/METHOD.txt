Roofing dataset — method (Isuru / checker Neal)
================================================

Why
---
Own local roofing dataset for 4 LGAs to (1) list roofing owners for fold-ins
and (2) area facts to rank hub location. Unique, checked, cleaned public data.

LGAs
----
Wollongong, Shellharbour, Shoalhaven (Nowra), Wingecarribee (Southern Highlands)

Rules
-----
- Public business data only
- No homeowner personal data, no bought lists
- Engineer never contacts businesses (outreach: Matt; send: Neal)
- Nic privacy check (APP + Spam Act) before list is used for outreach
- DataForSEO approved (Neal 6 Nov 2026) on group account with spend cap
- Google Places via official API only (backup)

Done line
---------
One Google Sheet: Businesses | Area demand | Roof signals | Map pack | Notes
Every row: source_link + collection_date. Neal sample-checks 20 rows.

Suburb driver
-------------
ABS ASGS Edition 3 correspondence CG_SAL_2021_LGA_2021.csv
Primary LGA where RATIO_FROM_TO >= 0.5
Built file: data/processed/suburb_list.csv

--------------------------------------------
1. scripts/build_suburb_list.py
2. scripts/init_sheet_bundle.py
3. scripts/search_businesses.py   [--provider dataforseo|places]
4. scripts/filter_non_roofers.py
5. scripts/enrich_abn.py
6. scripts/map_pack.py
